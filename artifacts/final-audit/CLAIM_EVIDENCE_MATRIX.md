# Claim–evidence matrix

Finalised on the Alien host. Columns follow the required schema:
Claim · Thesis location · Required evidence · Actual evidence · Reproducible? ·
Status.

- **Reproducible?** means: can a third party regenerate this from the repository
  on an independent host?
- **Status** is `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, or `UNSUPPORTED`.
- `tests/…` and `scripts/…` are re-runnable here. `testing-report/…` receipts are
  pinned historical records: their *derived* values are reproducible, their
  *execution* is not.

No claim classified `UNSUPPORTED` above is asserted as established anywhere in
`Thesis/Final/`; each is presented as a limitation or an explicit
non-claim. The consistency check `npm run test:thesis-numbers` and the boundary
check `npm run verify:claim-boundaries` exist to keep it that way.
