# Privacy Lens Thesis Revision 21 — Multidisciplinary Assessment

Date: 2026-08-11
App: 1.9.0 (version code 10)
Publication-target rubric: **92/100**

## Overall judgement

Revision 21 converts independent legal review from aspirational prose into a typed, testable release precondition for reassuring output. It does not claim that the current EU pack has been legally reviewed. The real pack remains an unattested technical candidate and the UI says so.

## Legal and regulatory perspective — 27/30

The attestation binds pack version, source-bundle digest, reviewer qualification, distinct approval, scope, time, and expiry. Missing, expired, mismatched, malformed, and self-approved states fail closed. This supports accountability-oriented governance. It does not authenticate people, qualifications, signatures, or source bytes. The distinct-approver rule is a project safeguard, not a statutory GDPR four-eyes requirement.

## Software and assurance perspective — 23/25

Deterministic positive and negative paths, 1,800 governance/property cases, mutation probes, compilation-before-test discipline, release builds, hashes, UI contracts, and physical installation form a coherent assurance chain. Remaining gaps include independent oracle diversity, cryptographic provenance, long-run reliability, broad-device coverage, and production signing.

## Interface and visual perspective — 20/20 within the rubric

The inspected OPPO captures show a prominent but non-alarmist gate, a compact legal-review chip, expanded assessment context, a specific missing-evidence statement, and a proportionate next action. Full internal-rubric credit does not mean accessibility certification or universal behaviour across devices, text scales, languages, and assistive technologies.

## User-psychology perspective — 13/15

The design avoids false reassurance while preserving useful technical signals. Synthetic provenance, source currency, legal-review status, uncertainty, and next action remain distinguishable. Participant evidence for comprehension, trust calibration, anxiety, and decision quality is still absent.

## Academic perspective — 9/10

The successor revision states its legal anchors, statutory boundary, falsifiable mechanism, adversarial cases, hashes, device evidence, and unresolved validity threats. Independent legal review, peer review, participant work, and external replication remain the main missing publication evidence.

## Recommended next iteration

Create a deterministic canonical source bundle, recompute its digest during verification, and add signed reviewer/approver assertions with revocation and independently controlled release authority. Do not award legal-validity credit until a qualified reviewer actually assesses the pack and evidence is independently verifiable.
