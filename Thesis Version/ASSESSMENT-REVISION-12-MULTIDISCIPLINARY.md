## What changed the score

1. A defensible design-science method replaced an informal iteration narrative.
2. Recent primary Android permission studies became part of the research gap and claim boundary.
3. A transparent source-quality protocol prevents the bibliography from being treated as an unqualified source list.
4. Conceptual, architecture, legal-traceability, implementation-witness, evaluation-triangulation, and assurance-case artefacts make the argument independently auditable.
5. Product qualities such as beautiful, trustworthy, and willing to use are explicitly retained as goals rather than reported as measured outcomes.

## Remaining blockers to 95+

1. Independent participant evidence for comprehension, task completion, perceived trust, accessibility, and willingness to use.
2. Independent legal review and a versioned pack approval/change-control record.
3. Mutation/property-based testing or an independent oracle that reduces correlated-defect risk.
4. Multi-device and assistive-technology evidence, including TalkBack and font scaling.
5. A baseline comparison and external replication or preregistered protocol.

## Iteration decision

Proceed to Revision 13. The next thesis-only iteration should deepen related-work comparison, formalise algorithmic and pack-governance reasoning, and add an examiner-facing claim-to-evidence matrix. It must not score itself above evidence that only code, devices, participants, or qualified legal review can supply. If Revision 13 remains below 95, strengthen the App and its evaluation evidence before revising the thesis again.
