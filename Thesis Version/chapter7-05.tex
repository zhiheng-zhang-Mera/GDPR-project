\subsection{August Revision Conclusion}

The August implementation closes the principal gap between the specified simulator and the Android code: randomised configurations now become bounded, delayed, locally recorded synthetic events and their progress is available to the application layer. The change strengthens reproducibility without disguising synthetic evidence as sensor activity.

The defensible conclusion remains limited. The framework demonstrates a transparent rule engine, controlled ground truth and an executable Android validation path. Production claims require Room-backed retention, authorised cross-application acquisition, physical-device 24-hour runs, measured energy use and independent legal and user evaluation.
