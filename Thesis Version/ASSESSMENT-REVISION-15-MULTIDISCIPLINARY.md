## Panel judgement

- **Law and governance:** the distinction among binding law, final guidance, and consultation material is now executable data. Article 6 basis evidence, Articles 13/14 transparency, Article 5 minimisation/retention, and Article 35 DPIA gaps are exposed without declaring an infringement. The pack remains a technical candidate.
- **Software engineering:** the candidate passes the fresh decision, mutation, governance, accessibility-source, release-privacy, Android build, package, and short physical-device checks recorded for version 1.3.0.
- **HCI and accessibility:** the current overview remains visually usable on the tested OPPO device, but Revision 15 deliberately spends no unearned participant or assistive-technology credit.
- **User psychology:** changing a verdict-like label to a potential-conflict escalation reduces automation-authority risk. Whether users understand or trust it appropriately is an empirical question reserved for a later study.
- **Research integrity:** the thesis distinguishes generated test accuracy from legal validity, a short device sample from reliability, and internal scoring from publication quality.

## Publication-position judgement

Revision 15 is closer to a strong Q2 engineering submission package because its legal claims are more traceable and falsifiable. It is not yet aligned with a publishable final submission: independent legal assessment, participant evidence, accessibility sessions, external replication, and peer-review response are still missing. These are score ceilings, not formatting tasks.
