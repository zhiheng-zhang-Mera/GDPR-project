## Novelty position

The matrix does not support a claim that Privacy Lens is the first Android policy language, GDPR analyzer, formal privacy system, or FlowDroid integration. Its narrower contribution is the integration boundary: heterogeneous technical evidence is admitted with type and provenance, transformed through executable temporal and safety semantics, interpreted by versioned regulation-owned obligations, and presented with missing evidence and prohibited inference intact. The evaluated system is differentiated by the continuity of that claim boundary across evidence types rather than by replacing the specialised analyses above.
