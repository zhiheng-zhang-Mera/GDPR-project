## RQ closure map

| Research question | Completed evidence classes | Principal claims | Residual boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1 — Safe deterministic semantics | Admission, adversarial, permutation, replay, overlap, restart and stress tests | C-ADM-01, C-TEMP-01, C-STATE-01 | Conformance to declared semantics is not field accuracy. |
| RQ2 — Evidence representation and calibrated presentation | Typed finding schema, policy fixtures, source contracts, recorded device render | C-POLICY-01, C-UI-01, C-NATIVE-01 | Implementation evidence is not participant comprehension. |
| RQ3 — Regulation-owned policy separation | Pack registry/compiler, cross-pack fixtures, finding history | C-PACK-01, C-POLICY-01 | Architecture does not validate legal mappings or new jurisdictions. |
| RQ4 — Evidence strength and external validity | F-Droid census, device denominator/failures, PSS missingness, FlowDroid interoperability, CI reproduction | C-FDROID-01, C-DEVICE-01, C-PSS-01, C-FLOW-01, C-REPRO-01 | Purposive open-source and single-device evidence does not support population, legal, human, or commercial claims. |
