# 4. Criterion 1 — Contribution

This is the highest-priority revision.

## B1. Create an explicit contribution model

The thesis must answer clearly:

> What did this project contribute that did not exist before?

Do not describe “the app” as the contribution.

Separate contributions into research contributions.

Recommended structure:

### C1 — Evidence-bounded privacy-review architecture

A system architecture that prevents technical privacy signals from automatically becoming legal conclusions.

### C2 — Regulation-driven temporal rule abstraction

Regulation-specific mappings are separated from the generic temporal evaluator so jurisdictional interpretation is not hard-coded into technical execution.

### C3 — Fail-closed governance chain

Unavailable provenance, review status, attestation, trust, rollback or policy evidence reduces available conclusions instead of silently producing reassurance.

### C4 — Typed separation between evidence, technical signal and legal conclusion

The architecture explicitly prevents certain classes of unsupported inference.

### C5 — Reproducible evaluation framework for claim boundaries

Tests evaluate not only whether detections fire but whether forbidden conclusions remain unreachable under malformed, adversarial and incomplete evidence.

These contributions must be tested against the literature.

Do not claim novelty merely because the implementation is new.

# 6. Criterion 3 — Research Method

This is the second major 90+ priority.

## D1. Introduce explicit Research Questions

If current RQs are weak or implicit, reformulate into approximately 3–5 clear RQs.

Candidate structure:

### RQ1

Can an Android privacy-review architecture preserve explicit boundaries between observed technical evidence and unsupported legal conclusions?

### RQ2

Can regulation-specific temporal mappings be separated from a generic evaluator while retaining traceability and conservative failure behaviour?

### RQ3

How reliably does the implementation preserve its specified safety properties under nominal, boundary, malformed and adversarial inputs?

### RQ4

What engineering evidence is obtainable from external Android application corpora and controlled physical-device workflows, and what claims remain unsupported?

### RQ5

How effectively does the prototype communicate uncertainty, evidence provenance and decision boundaries within the tested interface conditions?

Do not use an RQ whose answer is not supported by existing data.
