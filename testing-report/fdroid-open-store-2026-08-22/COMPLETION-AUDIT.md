This is a completion audit, not a claim that all research objectives have been achieved. It distinguishes reproducible engineering evidence from GDPR legal labels and comparative-performance evidence.

## Honest completion condition

The engineering and formal-model portions above are reproducible within their stated scope. The broader research claim remains open until an authorised commercial corpus and an independently labelled, same-corpus evaluation of Privacy Lens and each baseline are available. Until then, the defensible conclusion is that Privacy Lens provides regulation-owned, fail-closed advisory policy semantics complementary to existing static analyses; it has not been empirically shown to outperform them.
